Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit the site or contact Abdullah Haider.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the public portfolio at Creative Haider and inquiries submitted through its contact form. The site is operated by Abdullah Haider, based in Islamabad, Pakistan.

Information you choose to provide

The contact form asks for your first name, last name, email address, and message. Phone number, service, and budget are optional. When submitted, the selected service, budget, and phone number are included with the message so they can be reviewed together.

How inquiry information is used

Information submitted through the form is sent to the site’s Supabase-hosted data service and stored as an inquiry for follow-up through the site owner’s administrative inbox. It is used to understand and respond to your request, and to maintain relevant business correspondence. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through the form.

Service providers and links

The site is hosted on Vercel and uses Supabase to deliver published portfolio information and receive contact inquiries. These providers may process technical request information needed to operate their services under their own terms. Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising

The public pages were reviewed for this update. No analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing trackers were found in the site code. If those tools are added later, this policy and the cookie controls will be updated before they are used.

Retention and your choices

Inquiry details are kept while they are needed to respond to and manage the inquiry, maintain relevant business records, or meet applicable legal obligations. To ask about or request deletion of an inquiry, contact contact.abdullahhaider@gmail.com and include enough information to identify your request. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Children and policy updates

This portfolio and its services are intended for businesses and adults seeking professional services. This policy may be updated when the site’s data practices change; the date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact.abdullahhaider@gmail.com.
