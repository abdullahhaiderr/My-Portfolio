Cookie Policy

A clear summary of cookies, browser storage, and tracking on this site.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Public portfolio pages

The public portfolio pages do not currently use analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies, and the code reviewed for this update contains no analytics tags or advertising pixels. The public site does not need a cookie consent banner while no optional tracking is active.

Contact form and service requests

Submitting the contact form sends the details you provide to the site’s Supabase-hosted data service so the inquiry can be stored and answered. The form does not use an advertising or analytics cookie.

Administrative area

The site’s password-protected administrative area may use essential browser storage to maintain an administrator’s authenticated session. This storage supports the dashboard and is not used to track public visitors or serve advertising.

External websites

If you follow a link to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or another external website, that provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Their policies apply after you leave this site.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking is introduced, this policy will be updated and an appropriate consent choice will be offered before that tracking is activated where required.
